# Caça ao Preço

- What it is: a free WhatsApp group (plus the Instagram @caca_ao_preco_) that posts curated Shopee deals with Shopee affiliate links; the founders earn the Shopee affiliate commission when a member buys through the link.
- Who it is for: ordinary Brazilian workers, men and women, formally employed (CLT), earning about R$ 2,000 to R$ 3,000 a month, who buy on impulse for the home, for their kids and for themselves.
- What it sells, at what price: nothing is sold directly; members join and use it for free. Products posted cost up to R$ 300, with at least 30% real discount. Revenue = Shopee affiliate commission (rate varies per product, shown in the Shopee affiliate panel).
- Where and how: online, Brazil. Instagram stories (recorded by Yara) and, soon, paid Meta ads for a specific product drive people into the WhatsApp group, where they can buy that product through the affiliate link. Group posts 4 rounds a day (7h beauty, 12h home, 18h kids, 21h men's), 3 posts per round.
- Budget and constraints: run by two founders (Tito and Yara), no limit on hours. Ad budget not defined yet. Today: 50 members in the group, R$ 0 per month in commission, organic only, paid traffic about to start. Goal: R$ 20,000 per month.
